Style ideas
Suggestion
The tonal edit
Pick three pieces from the same colour family — beige, camel, sand — and layer textures instead of colours.
Tip
One statement, rest quiet
Let a single piece do the talking. Bold colour, strong silhouette, or standout texture — then neutralise everything else.
Insight
The rule of thirds
Break your outfit into visual thirds: top, middle, and bottom. An unexpected break — a tucked shirt, a belt, a cropped layer — creates effortless proportion.
